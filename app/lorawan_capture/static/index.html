Gateways

Gateway reports

From the gateways' own stats messages. Bad CRC: received but not decodable. Stored: share of the good packets a gateway reported that reached this logger (counted between reports that both arrived, so a restart does not skew it).

Own devices

Share of each device's uplinks (counted from its frame counter, leaving out times nothing was recorded) that reached each gateway. The gateways only see a device's DevAddr, and an OTAA device gets a new one every time it joins. Add a device by its DevEUI (or give an address its DevEUI) and it is followed: when it joins again, the address its join leads to is taken over, with the same name. Kept in devices.json.

No own devices yet: add one with its DevEUI or DevAddr (e.g. from ChirpStack, or from the Devices tab).

Channels

Packets per frequency and gateway.

Channel occupancy

How much of each hour a channel was on air, as one gateway heard it: the airtime of the packets it decoded, worked out from their length, spreading factor and bandwidth (an estimate: other systems may use longer preambles). Signals a gateway cannot decode, like the RFID reader, do not show here. Local time.
